## Cutout extraction and repair

Each approved master was submitted separately with a background-extraction direction that locked identity, pose, proportions, clothing, equipment, colors, subject lighting, scale, framing, and full silhouette while requesting genuine alpha. The tool returned RGB checkerboard renders. `scripts/repair_checker_alpha.py` removed boundary-connected pale low-chroma backdrop pixels; Cal Harrow also required a visual-QA pass that removed large enclosed checker components between his rake and clothing. Every result was conformed to its 1024×1536 master canvas, optimized as an indexed 8-bit PNG with `tRNS`, and visually checked over a light-to-dark field for transparent corners, substantial background transparency, non-empty silhouette, and clean prop gaps.

```text
Use case: background-extraction
Asset type: Sable Reach transparent named-NPC cutout for <CHARACTER>
Input images: Image 1 is the sole edit target and identity source.
Primary request: Remove only the near-black studio background and floor, returning a clean full-body cutout on a genuinely transparent background.
Constraints: preserve the exact character identity, face, expression, pose, anatomy, clothing, equipment, hands, boots, subject lighting, colors, scale, canvas, and complete silhouette. Preserve fine hair, reed, rope, metal, and frayed cloth edges. Four corners must be transparent. No baked checkerboard, matte rectangle, halo, crop, restyling, added or removed object, text, logo, or watermark.
```
