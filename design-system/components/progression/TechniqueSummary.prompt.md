Header for a discipline's technique tree.

```jsx
<TechniqueSummary icon="swordsmanship" level={41} tier="Adept"
  description="Swordsmanship records what you have actually swung, not what you carry." />
```

Sits under CodexBack, above the technique grid.

## Accessibility

The glyph is decorative. Place the component under a real discipline heading supplied by the page.
