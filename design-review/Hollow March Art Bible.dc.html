The rule

Every subject, and which gap it is sitting in

Every subject on this page carries two columns: the concept art, and what the canonical index knows about the subject. No render appears in either — the rig kit is humanoid-only and nothing here has authority to check a render against, so putting one on the page would be the exact failure the law names. Where no concept art exists the column is not quietly dropped; it is drawn as an ember-marked gap.

Concept art is graded as reference — sepia(.18) saturate(.7) contrast(1.18), the atlas-still grade — and it is applied to every plate on this page. Nothing is shown raw; the character grade stays reserved for rendered output, so the day a render appears beside a plate the two are told apart without a label.

04b

Rank 1 · authored this pass · hand to ChatGPT

Seven prompts, so the art can exist at all

{{ promptStat }}

Batch 01 covered bestiary families only, so every named character had no rank-1 authority whatsoever — nothing approved, nothing to conform to. Authoring the prompt is the one part of this I can do without seeing anything: the prompt is design intent in words, and writing it is what rank 1 is. Each follows batch-01 form exactly, repeats the common-law presentation rules verbatim, and carries one load-bearing detail the render is judged on first.

One deliberate departure, stated so it is not mistaken for drift: these are living people, so no anatomical impossibility is requested. The load-bearing detail is instead evidence of a life — a hip that failed and was never set, a rope scar across both palms, a shoulder an inch higher than the other. The four readable origin plates set that precedent: ordinary anatomy carrying {{ burdenLine }}.
